Traditional Beshbarmak Recipe

Description

Beshbarmak is the national dish of Kazakhstan, consisting of tender, slow-cooked meat served over wide, homemade flat noodles, accompanied by an onion broth sauce. Its name translates to "five fingers" because it is traditionally eaten by hand.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg beef or lamb brisket with bones
  • 3 liters water
  • 2 yellow onions (one whole, one cut into thin half-rings)
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 10 whole black peppercorns
  • Salt to taste
  • 400 g all-purpose flour (for noodles)
  • 1 egg (for noodles)
  • 150 ml water or meat broth (for noodles)

Instructions

  1. Place the bone-in meat, whole onion, bay leaves, and peppercorns into a large pot with 3 liters of cold water.
  2. Bring the water to a boil, skim off any foam from the surface, season with salt, cover, and simmer on low heat for 2.5 to 3 hours until the meat falls off the bone.
  3. Remove the cooked meat, shred it into bite-sized pieces, and set it aside. Strain the remaining broth and keep it hot.
  4. Mix the flour, egg, salt, and water in a bowl to form a stiff dough, then cover and let it rest for 30 minutes.
  5. Roll the dough out very thinly on a floured surface and cut it into diamond or square shapes (about 5x5 cm).
  6. Place the sliced onion rings into a small pot, pour 250 ml of the hot meat broth over them, and boil for 2 to 3 minutes until softened, then season with ground black pepper to create the sauce.
  7. Bring the main pot of filtered broth back to a rolling boil and cook the dough squares for 5 to 7 minutes until tender.
  8. Arrange the cooked noodles on a large serving platter, top with the shredded meat and the onion sauce, and serve with bowls of hot broth on the side.
