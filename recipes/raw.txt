Traditional Beshbarmak Recipe
Description
Beshbarmak is the national dish of Kazakhstan, consisting of tender, slow-cooked meat served over wide, homemade flat noodles, accompanied by an onion broth sauce. Its name translates to "five fingers" because it was traditionally eaten by hand.

Ingredients
1.5 kg beef or lamb brisket with bones

3 liters water

2 yellow onions (one whole, one cut into thin half-rings)

2 bay leaves

10 whole black peppercorns

Salt to taste

400 g all-purpose flour (for noodles)

1 egg (for noodles)

150 ml water or meat broth (for noodles)

Instructions
Place the bone-in meat, whole onion, bay leaves, and peppercorns into a large pot with 3 liters of cold water.

Bring the water to a boil, skim off any foam from the surface, season with salt, cover, and simmer on low heat for 2.5 to 3 hours until the meat falls off the bone.

Remove the cooked meat, shred it into bite-sized pieces, and set it aside. Strain the remaining broth and keep it hot.

Mix the flour, egg, salt, and water in a bowl to form a stiff dough, then cover and let it rest for 30 minutes.

Roll the dough out very thinly on a floured surface and cut it into diamond or square shapes (about 5x5 cm).

Place the sliced onion rings into a small pot, pour 250 ml of the hot meat broth over them, and boil for 2 to 3 minutes until softened, then season with ground black pepper to create the sauce.

Bring the main pot of filtered broth back to a rolling boil and cook the dough squares for 5 to 7 minutes until tender.

Arrange the cooked noodles on a large serving platter, top with the shredded meat and the onion sauce, and serve with bowls of hot broth on the side.

Classic Egg Fried Rice Recipe
Description
Egg fried rice is a quick, flavorful staple dish made by stir-frying cooked rice with eggs, aromatics, and savory seasonings. Using day-old cold rice yields the best texture, keeping the grains separate and fluffy.

Ingredients
4 cups cooked cold jasmine or long-grain rice (preferably day-old)

3 tablespoons cooking oil, divided

4 large eggs, beaten with a pinch of salt

2 tablespoons soy sauce

4 green onions, sliced

Salt and ground black pepper to taste

Instructions
Heat a wok or large skillet over high heat, add 1 tablespoon of cooking oil, and swirl to coat the surface.

Pour the beaten eggs into the hot wok and scramble them quickly until they are about three-quarters cooked.

Add the remaining 2 tablespoons of cooking oil and the cold cooked rice directly into the wok with the eggs.

Break down any clumps of rice using a spatula and toss continuously to mix the rice thoroughly with the eggs.

Drizzle the soy sauce around the inner edges of the hot wok so it sizzles and imparts a smoky umami flavor to the rice.

Season with salt and pepper to taste, continuing to stir-fry everything together for another 2 to 3 minutes until the rice is heated through and slightly caramelized.

Turn off the heat, stir in the fresh sliced green onions, and serve immediately.
