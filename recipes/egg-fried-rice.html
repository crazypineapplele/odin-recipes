Classic Egg Fried Rice Recipe

Description

Egg fried rice is a quick, flavorful staple dish made by stir-frying cooked rice with eggs, aromatics, and savory seasonings. Using day-old cold rice yields the best texture, keeping the grains separate and fluffy.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups cooked cold jasmine or long-grain rice (preferably day-old)
  • 3 tablespoons cooking oil, divided
  • 4 large eggs, beaten with a pinch of salt
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 4 green onions, sliced
  • Salt and ground black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat a wok or large skillet over high heat, add 1 tablespoon of cooking oil, and swirl to coat the surface.
  2. Pour the beaten eggs into the hot wok and scramble them quickly until they are about three-quarters cooked.
  3. Add the remaining 2 tablespoons of cooking oil and the cold cooked rice directly into the wok with the eggs.
  4. Break down any clumps of rice using a spatula and toss continuously to mix the rice thoroughly with the eggs.
  5. Drizzle the soy sauce around the inner edges of the hot wok so it sizzles and imparts a smoky umami flavor to the rice.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste, continuing to stir-fry everything together for another 2 to 3 minutes until the rice is heated through and slightly caramelized.
  7. Turn off the heat, stir in the fresh sliced green onions, and serve immediately.
